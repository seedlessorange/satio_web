Questions

Frequently asked

When is Satio launching?

We're targeting Q2 2026 for our public launch on Android. iOS will follow approximately 6 months later. Waitlist members will get early access before the public launch.

Is Satio free?

Satio will be a subscription app with a 14-day free trial. Founding members who join the waitlist will receive special pricing and extended trials. We don't have ads and never will.

How does the AI work?

You take a photo of your meal, and our AI (powered by Claude) identifies the food, estimates portions and nutrition, and logs it automatically. It learns your preferences over time to get more accurate and provide personalized coaching.

What if I don't want to see calories?

That's exactly why we built Mindful mode. It removes all numerical data and focuses entirely on how you feel hunger cues, fullness, and your relationship with food. No judgment, no numbers.

How is this different from MyFitnessPal?

MyFitnessPal requires manual entry, shows guilt-inducing red numbers, and treats everyone the same. Satio uses AI to log meals from photos, adapts to your comfort level with data, and provides supportive coaching instead of judgment.
