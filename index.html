Questions

Frequently asked questions

Q When is Satio launching?

Satio launches in Q2 2026. Join the waitlist for early access before the public launch.

Q Is Satio free?

Satio has a free tier with manual logging, barcode scanning, search, and basic tracking. AI features like photo logging, pattern recognition, and personalized insights require premium after your trial ends. We want to be upfront: AI costs money to run.

Q How does the AI work?

Satio uses AI to analyze your photos, understand your patterns, and connect food with mood, sleep, and other factors. It learns from every meal you log.

Q What if I don't want to see calories?

Use Mindful Mode for zero numbers. Track how you feel instead. You choose your comfort level.

Q Can I connect my fitness tracker?

Yes. Satio integrates with Apple Watch, Fitbit, Garmin, and other fitness trackers for a complete picture.

Q Is my data private?

Yes. Your data is encrypted and never sold. When you use AI features, we send meal photos and context to our AI (Claude by Anthropic) but never your name, email, or identifying information. The AI sees "a 35-year-old trying to lose weight" not "John Smith from Paris." You own your data and you decide what to share.
