Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

At Satio, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

Waitlist: When you join our waitlist, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Referral information (if you were referred or refer others)
  • Timestamp of signup

App (when launched): The Satio app will collect:

  • Account information (email, name)
  • Meal photos and food logs
  • Health data you choose to share (sleep, mood, exercise)
  • Usage analytics

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and improve our service
  • To send you updates about Satio (you can unsubscribe anytime)
  • To process AI-powered nutrition insights
  • To track referrals and rewards

3. AI and Your Data

When you use AI features, we send meal photos and context to our AI provider (Claude by Anthropic). We never send personally identifiable information like your name or email to the AI. The AI sees "a 35-year-old trying to lose weight" not "John Smith."

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We only share data with:

  • Service providers who help us operate Satio (hosting, email)
  • AI providers for nutrition analysis (without identifying info)
  • Legal authorities if required by law

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption and security practices. Your data is stored securely on Firebase/Google Cloud infrastructure.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Delete your data
  • Export your data
  • Unsubscribe from emails

7. Contact Us

For privacy questions, email us at: privacy@satio.app

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We'll notify you of significant changes via email.
